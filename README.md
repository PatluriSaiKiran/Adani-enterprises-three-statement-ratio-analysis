# Adani Enterprises Ltd: Three-Statement and Ratio Analysis

## Overview

This project presents a financial statement analysis of Adani Enterprises Ltd, an Indian listed conglomerate. It covers ten financial years (FY2017 to FY2026) and combines the income statement, balance sheet and cash flow statement with a set of financial ratios built in Excel.

All figures are in ₹ crore unless stated otherwise.

## Objectives

- Build a linked three-statement model from published financial data.
- Assess growth, profitability, leverage and cash generation over ten years.
- Examine how the company has funded its capital expenditure.

## Data Source

Financial data was extracted from [Screener.in](https://www.screener.in) (annual and quarterly statements, balance sheet and cash flow). The data is used for educational and portfolio purposes only.

## Workbook Structure

| Sheet | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| `Data Sheet` | Raw annual and quarterly data from Screener |
| `Profit & Loss` | Screener's summary profit and loss view |
| `Cash_Flow_Data` | Detailed cash flow items |
| `HistoricalIFS` | Historical income statement, balance sheet and cash flow statement |
| `Ratio_Analysis` | Growth, margin and return ratios |

## Methodology

1. **Data extraction:** Downloaded ten years of annual statements and recent quarterly results from Screener.
2. **Statement build:** Linked the raw data to a structured income statement, balance sheet and cash flow statement, including a balance sheet check (total assets equal total liabilities).
3. **Ratio analysis:** Calculated the following groups of ratios:
   - Growth: sales, EBITDA, EBIT, net profit and dividend growth
   - Profitability: gross, EBITDA, EBIT, EBT and net margins
   - Cost structure: selling and administrative expenses and depreciation as a percentage of sales
   - Returns: return on capital employed, return on equity, retention and self-sustained growth ratio

## Observations from the Source Data

- Sales grew from ₹36,533 crore in FY17 to ₹100,469 crore in FY26. Growth was uneven, with a sharp rise in FY22 and FY23 followed by a fall in FY24.
- Net profit rose from ₹988 crore in FY17 to ₹9,339 crore in FY26.
- Other income reached ₹11,688 crore in FY26, which is close to the level of operating profit (₹13,991 crore). Reported profit before tax therefore depends heavily on non-operating items.
- Borrowings increased from ₹20,846 crore in FY17 to ₹106,622 crore in FY26.
- In FY26, cash from operations was ₹2,357 crore, while investing outflows were ₹27,680 crore. The gap was met mainly by financing inflows of ₹28,484 crore, which indicates that expansion is being funded largely by external capital.

## Limitations

- The analysis relies on data from a single secondary source and has not been reconciled with the company's annual reports.
